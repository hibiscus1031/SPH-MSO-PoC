# SPH-MSO-PoC

Qualification-first proof of concept for multiscale state and operator observability in structure-preserving smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH).

## Scientific scope and status

SPH-MSO asks whether deployment-compatible responses of the same frozen SPH semidiscrete operator at multiple compact-support scales provide information about instantaneous spatial discretization defects that is missing from a paired single-scale representation. The formal comparison holds cases, particles, targets, physics, folds, normalization, and statistical aggregation fixed while changing only the representation from SS39 to MS110.

The frozen project conclusion is `PROJECT_SUPPORT_SCALE_MULTISCALE_ROUTE_CLOSED_PUBLICATION_EVIDENCE_FROZEN`. The original H-MSO-01 test remains `NOT_EVALUABLE` because its registered Descriptor Nearest-Neighbour statistic encountered a structural zero-denominator singularity. A new prospective, zero-safe H-MSO-01R test qualified density-rate defect identifiability, while pressure and viscosity did not qualify local operational-identifiability rescue despite substantial frozen cross-fitted oracle improvements. Target-blind geometry realignment and the evaluated direction-resolved algebraic reparameterizations did not establish an actionable continuation route.

This is a component-dependent and scope-bounded result. It is not a claim of intrinsic mathematical non-identifiability, a failure of all metrics, a requirement for temporal or directional information, or an impossibility result for Transformers, neural SPH, learned operators, or separately contracted supervised representations.

## Evidence and reproducibility boundary

The committed manifests, status ledgers, reports, registries, and serialized artifacts are the scientific sources of truth. Their evidence roles must remain distinct: fresh confirmatory evidence may carry a prospective verdict, while consumed diagnostic evidence may explain or bound that verdict but may not rewrite it.

No fresh case, target, or reference generation is authorized. Do not run scientific scripts, regenerate metrics, redraw bootstrap samples, train a model, or open a new hypothesis as routine repository validation. Publication transformations may reorganize already frozen values and statuses only under the rules in `publication/final_claim_freeze.md` and `publication/figure_table_plan.md`.

The repository is a frozen scientific evidence root rather than a packaged software release. No standalone project-scoped dependency lock or cross-platform installation claim is provided.

## Qualification philosophy

Numerical admissibility, metric evaluability, operational identifiability, global prediction, and route actionability are separate gates. Passing an earlier gate never implies a later one. Coverage is geometry evidence rather than proof of identifiability, and oracle improvement is not proof that all relevant information is present.

The publication route is a qualification-first numerical-methodology study centered on the paired SS/MS intervention, the retained density-rate qualification, the pressure/viscosity confirmatory negative evidence, and the empirical separation between global predictability and local operational identifiability. It is not a successful neural-solver narrative.

## Relationship to SPH-DDO and SPH-PIO

SPH-DDO supplies historical motivation, target definitions, and a read-only analytical/provenance boundary; its H3 outcomes are not the MSO single-scale control or MSO scientific evidence. SPH-PIO supplies the provenance-audited, byte-identical Stage01C static SPH operator subset; its architecture and training outcomes are not MSO evidence. The three projects retain independent scientific evidence chains and must not repackage the same numerical experiment as separate primary evidence.
