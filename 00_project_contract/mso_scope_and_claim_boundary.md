# MSO scope and claim boundary

## In scope

MSO tests whether explicitly exposing the response of a frozen, deployment-available SPH semidiscrete operator to support-scale variation makes the already-frozen DDO instantaneous spatial defect less conditionally ambiguous than a paired single-scale representation.

The intended scientific sequence is:

1. qualify scale evaluations without target access;
2. compare fresh paired SS and MS representations with non-neural identifiability diagnostics;
3. consider a structured learned correction only if the prospective absolute and relative gates pass in a separately authorized stage.

## What differs from DDO

DDO asked whether a single-scale online-observable representation was sufficient and found that its tested route was not supported. MSO preserves the DDO target but prospectively varies the **representation of numerical scale response**. DDO outcomes are historical context, never MSO controls or results.

## What differs from PIO

PIO studied verification-first structure-preserving particle interactions, learned conservative corrections, optimizer paths, training campaigns, and solver qualification. MSO-00 selects no neural architecture and performs no training or rollout. PIO can supply only provenance-audited static SPH/operator interfaces and transferable governance concepts; its predictive results are not MSO evidence.

## Allowed claim if H-MSO-01 passes

“On the prospectively frozen fresh atlas, for the frozen DDO defect and qualified SPH operator, the registered multiscale-response representation reduced conditional ambiguity relative to its paired single-scale control and met the preregistered absolute identifiability gates.”

This claim is componentwise and support-limited. It is not proof of global mathematical identifiability, well-posedness, learnability by a neural architecture, rollout stability, or superiority at a different resolution/model/kernel.

## Claims explicitly prohibited

MSO must not claim:

- first use of attention for particle fluids;
- first multiscale neural CFD model;
- first learned SPH kernel;
- first learned mesh-free operator;
- first differentiable SPH plus ML;
- that multiscale representation alone guarantees accuracy, conservation, stability, generalization, or rollout success;
- that DDO failure is an MSO result or that historical DDO numbers are the SS arm;
- that PIO training evidence validates MSO;
- that any ARC evidence, parameters, or qualification enters MSO;
- that the Deng–Hani–Ma hard-sphere/Boltzmann/fluid limits prove SPH scale-response well-posedness;
- that a change of support radius is a change of particle resolution;
- that the first MS representation learns or modifies \(W\).

## Architecture and model boundary

No MLP, GNN, Transformer, attention architecture, learned kernel, learned \(W\), D1/D2/D3 PIO architecture, optimizer, temporal loss, or correction parameterization is selected or authorized by MSO-00. Simple non-neural oracles are diagnostics only.

## Evidence boundary

Only a future, fresh, manifest-bound, paired SS/MS experiment can answer H-MSO-01. Literature supports positioning; DDO/PIO support definitions, provenance, and governance; MSO-01 supports only numerical admissibility of the candidate ladder. None of these substitutes for fresh target evidence.
