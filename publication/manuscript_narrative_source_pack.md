# Manuscript narrative source pack

## Working title

**From resolvable multiscale numerical responses to component-dependent defect identifiability: global predictability does not imply local identifiability in structure-preserving SPH correction**

## One-sentence thesis

Prospectively exposing a frozen structure-preserving SPH operator at multiple support scales qualified density-rate defect identifiability, but not pressure or viscosity defect identifiability, even though a frozen cross-fitted non-neural oracle improved for all three components; the result separates global predictive structure from local operational identifiability within the tested deployment-observable representation.

## Recommended paper identity

- **Paper type:** numerical-methodology and negative-results study with one retained positive component result.
- **Main object:** prelearning qualification of a representation for instantaneous SPH spatial-discretization defects.
- **Not the object:** a successful learned solver, neural closure, Transformer, learned kernel, time integrator or rollout.
- **Primary contrast:** paired same-state SS39 versus MS110 deployment-observable representations.
- **Meaning of DNN:** Descriptor Nearest-Neighbour statistic, not deep neural network.
- **Inferential unit:** frozen case/lineage-aware, fold/family-balanced design; particle rows are not independent scientific replicates.

## Abstract source skeleton

**Problem.** Deployment-observable features can support global prediction while still failing to organize nearby states by similar discretization defect. Whether explicit support-scale responses reduce this conditional ambiguity should therefore be qualified before a learned correction is built.

**Design.** We froze a structure-preserving SPH operator, a target-blind support-scale ladder, a paired single-scale/multiscale representation, fresh lineage-held-out atlases, and componentwise absolute and paired relative gates. An original descriptor-neighbour statistic became statistically non-evaluable because of structural zero denominators; it was not called a scientific failure. A new prospective fresh test used the aggregate zero-safe Candidate C statistic without altering the other gates.

**Results.** In fresh H-MSO-01R evidence, the density-rate component passed Candidate C, conditional-variance and oracle absolute/relative gates. Pressure and viscosity did not qualify local operational-identifiability rescue, although their frozen cross-fitted oracle NRMSE improved across all six folds and four families. Consumed diagnostics did not establish an alternative target-blind geometry or an incremental-information direction proxy; global/local separation was the dominant supported mechanism.

**Conclusion.** Resolvable multiscale numerical responses can improve operational identifiability in a component-dependent way. Under the frozen representation, global predictive improvement was insufficient to establish local identifiability for the two momentum components. The tested support-scale prelearning route is therefore closed and packaged for publication, without a neural-training stage.

## Results architecture

### Result 1 — The support-scale intervention was numerically testable

MSO-01 qualified the registered 0.75/1.00/1.25/1.50 ladder using target-blind identity, support, topology, determinism, structure and resolvability checks. This supports the sentence “the numerical response was resolvable,” not “the defect was identifiable.”

### Result 2 — The original formal statistic was not evaluable

MSO-02B encountered structural zero denominators in the originally registered Descriptor Nearest-Neighbour statistic. Because that statistic was a mandatory gate, H-MSO-01 is permanently `NOT_EVALUABLE`. The non-DNN metrics cannot be promoted to substitute endpoints.

### Result 3 — A zero-safe amendment enabled a genuinely new prospective test

MSO-02C G1 attributed the singularity using consumed evidence without changing the old verdict. MSO-02C G2 selected Candidate C on synthetic fixtures: registered weighted sums form `W(N)` and `W(B)` before one final division. H-MSO-01R-A then froze a new historically nonoverlapping atlas and analysis before any R-B target access.

### Result 5 — Global prediction and local operational identifiability separated

For both momentum components, the oracle improvement replicated in 6/6 folds and 4/4 families, whereas Candidate C and CVAR did not satisfy relative rescue. This is empirical evidence that a global predictor can exploit structure that the frozen local-neighbour geometry does not turn into target-similar neighborhoods. It does not prove that all relevant information is present, or that an unrestricted learner would recover it.

### Result 6 — No target-blind continuation met the prospective-action threshold

MSO-02D did not establish a unique alternative target-blind geometry (Route A). The 70 computable direction-resolved proxies were algebraic reparameterizations of the existing 110D representation, and the registered new-information P5 proxy was unavailable and unevaluated (Route B). F-MS6 was `SUPPORTED_DOMINANT`; Route C recommended closure.

## Discussion architecture

1. **Positive component result:** Explicit scale response can reduce local conditional ambiguity, demonstrated by density-rate qualification.
2. **Component dependence:** The same intervention need not transfer from a scalar continuity-related defect to vector momentum-related defects.
3. **Global/local distinction:** Cross-fitted oracle performance and local-neighbour identifiability answer different questions and should not be used interchangeably.
4. **Coverage distinction:** In-distribution feature-space coverage is not evidence that nearby observables have nearby targets.
5. **Metric discipline:** A singular registered statistic creates `NOT_EVALUABLE`; a fresh zero-safe amendment is required before a scientific pass/fail can be issued.
6. **Negative-route value:** Failure to establish a target-blind next intervention is itself a publication-relevant closure result when the positive density finding is preserved.
7. **Literature boundary:** Learned SPH, neural particle simulation, learned mesh-free operators and particle attention already exist; this paper's contribution is the qualification workflow and component-resolved evidence, not invention of those model families.

## Final allowed core claims

A. Qualified multiscale support-scale numerical responses substantially improved operational identifiability of density-rate discretization defects within the tested scope.

B. For pressure and viscosity defects, multiscale observables substantially improved frozen cross-fitted oracle prediction but did not qualify local operational-identifiability rescue.

C. Global predictive performance and local operational identifiability are empirically separable properties under the frozen deployment-observable representation.

D. Feature-space coverage is not sufficient evidence for defect identifiability.

E. Target-blind geometry realignment and evaluated direction-resolved algebraic reparameterizations did not establish an actionable rescue route.

## Mandatory wording boundaries

Use “operational identifiability under the frozen representation and gates,” not “intrinsic mathematical identifiability.” Use “oracle prediction improved,” not an explained-variance transformation. Use “no actionable route was established,” not “all metrics fail.” Use “the project did not test a Transformer or neural solver,” not “a Transformer or neural SPH cannot work.” Use “P5 was unavailable and unevaluated,” not “P5 would solve the problem.”

## Limitations that must remain visible

- The conclusion is restricted to the frozen instantaneous spatial defect, operator, atlases, scale ladder, SS39/MS110 representations, metrics and gates.
- F4 has few independent lineages; particle counts must not be described as independent replication.
- MSO-02D is consumed diagnostic evidence and did not re-verdict H-MSO-01R.
- Directional association from algebraic reparameterizations is not incremental information.
- No temporal/history input, supervised representation learning, attention, Transformer, learned operator, time integration or solver rollout was evaluated.
- The literature refresh is a primary-source claim-boundary check, not an exhaustive systematic review.
