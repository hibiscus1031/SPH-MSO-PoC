# Final failure taxonomy

The word “failure” is overloaded. This taxonomy fixes which object failed, which status follows, and which stronger conclusion is prohibited.

| Failure class | Formal object | Operational test | MSO observation | Correct status/consequence | Does **not** imply |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Numerical admissibility failure | Candidate support-scale numerical response | Identity, support/topology, determinism, structural and resolvability gates | Not observed for the frozen 0.75/1.00/1.25/1.50 ladder | MSO-01 qualified; later identifiability testing became eligible | Identifiability is guaranteed |
| Metric evaluability failure | Registered statistic and its denominator/aggregation | Statistic must be finite and defined under all frozen zero semantics | Original particlewise DNN encountered structural zero denominators | H-MSO-01 and MSO-02B remain permanently `NOT_EVALUABLE` | Scientific `FAIL`; absence of predictive information |
| Operational identifiability failure | Componentwise mapping under the frozen deployment-observable representation and local geometry | All mandatory absolute gates must be evaluable and pass | In fresh R-B, pressure and viscosity were evaluable but failed; density passed | Momentum components `H_MSO01R_COMPONENT_NOT_QUALIFIED`; density `..._QUALIFIED` | Intrinsic mathematical non-identifiability |
| Relative rescue failure | Paired MS versus SS improvement under preregistered point/UCB thresholds | Candidate C, CVAR and oracle relative gates | Pressure/viscosity oracle passed, but Candidate C/CVAR did not; density passed all | Global H-MSO-01R `NOT_QUALIFIED` | No useful global predictive structure |
| Target-blind geometry failure | A preregisterable alternate representation geometry selected without target use | Stability, replication and selection criteria for U1–U3 | No unique candidate was established | Route A `NOT_SUPPORTED` | Every Euclidean/non-Euclidean metric must fail |
| Direction-proxy incremental-information failure | Deployment-compatible direction-resolved proxy with information beyond the 110D representation | O(2) validity, zero semantics, cross-fold/family replication, incremental information and density guard | 70 computable proxies were algebraic reparameterizations; P5 new information was unavailable/unevaluated | Route B `NOT_SUPPORTED` | Directional information is necessary; P5 would solve the problem |
| Global-versus-local separation | Relation between cross-fitted prediction and local-neighbour ambiguity | Oracle gains compared with Candidate C/CVAR and coverage diagnostics | Momentum oracle improved in 6/6 folds and 4/4 families while local rescue did not qualify; density improved jointly | F-MS6 `SUPPORTED_DOMINANT` | All information is proven present; a particular learner will succeed |
| Project route closure | Existence of an actionable target-blind continuation within the support-scale prelearning route | Route A or B must support a single prospective fresh intervention | Neither route met its contract; no H-MSO-02 candidate was established | Route C `SUPPORT_SCALE_ROUTE_CLOSURE_RECOMMENDED`; MSO-02Z freezes publication evidence | Universal closure of neural SPH, supervised learning, temporal modeling or all future science |

## Decision rules

1. `NOT_EVALUABLE` is used when the registered test cannot yield its required formal statistic; it is not numerically ordered with PASS/FAIL.
2. `NOT_QUALIFIED` is used when an evaluable prospective test fails one or more mandatory gates.
3. Component qualification is retained even when a conjunctive global hypothesis is not qualified.
4. Consumed diagnostic evidence may support a mechanism label or route decision but cannot rewrite the prospective result.
5. `SUPPORTED_DOMINANT` means best supported among the registered MSO-02D mechanisms under frozen diagnostics; it is not a mathematical uniqueness or causal-identification result.

## Project-specific sequence

`numerically admissible` → `original metric not evaluable` → `zero-safe new prospective test` → `density operationally qualified / momentum not qualified` → `global/local separation supported` → `no actionable target-blind route` → `support-scale route closed for publication`.
