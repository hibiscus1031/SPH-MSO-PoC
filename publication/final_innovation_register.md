## Literature-conditioned novelty boundary

The literature already contains learned SPH corrections, differentiable SPH, neural SPH simulators, momentum-conserving particle networks, multiscale graph models, learned mesh-free differential operators, point-cloud neural operators and local/global particle attention. The defensible contribution is therefore the specific frozen evidence architecture and the resulting component-dependent operational-identifiability finding—not invention of learning on particles, multiscale particle models, learned kernels/operators, attention, or Transformers.
