## Result hierarchy

| Layer | Question answered | Frozen answer | What it cannot answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numerical admissibility | Are support-scale responses stable and resolvable enough to test? | Yes, for the registered ladder | Whether defects are identifiable |
| Metric evaluability | Does the registered statistic define a valid formal test? | Original DNN: no; Candidate C in fresh R-B: yes | Whether an unevaluable old test failed scientifically |
| Operational identifiability | Does the deployed representation produce target-similar local neighborhoods and pass all gates? | Density: yes; pressure/viscosity: no | Intrinsic mathematical identifiability of the continuum problem |
| Global prediction | Can a frozen cross-fitted non-neural oracle predict from the representation? | Improved for all three components | Local operational rescue or information completeness |
| Future route | Did target-blind geometry or new-information proxy diagnostics establish a preregistrable rescue? | Route A/B not supported | Whether a separately designed supervised project could succeed |

## Claim transfer rules

1. Fresh confirmatory evidence carries hypothesis verdicts only when the corresponding prospective metric is evaluable.
2. Consumed diagnostics may explain or bound a frozen result but may not reselect features, change gates, or re-verdict it.
3. Coverage is an input-geometry condition, not a substitute for target-identifiability evidence.
4. Oracle improvement is evidence of global predictive structure, not proof of local operational identifiability or complete information.
5. Closure applies to the tested support-scale prelearning route. It is not a universal statement about neural SPH, Transformers, temporal models or all representations.
